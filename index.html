Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

POLARITY: MAGNETIC RIFT (com.games.PolarityMR.Android)
Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Overview & Information Collection

POLARITY: MAGNETIC RIFT does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No User Accounts: The application does not require user registration, sign-up, email addresses, names, or passwords.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: We do not request or access contacts, microphone, camera, external storage files, or device location.

2. On-Device Local Data Storage

All gameplay progression is stored exclusively on your device within Android's sandboxed private storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Campaign sector progression, solved chambers, and star ratings
  • Custom puzzle blueprints crafted in the Sandbox Level Editor
  • Sound, soundtrack volume, and visual preferences
  • Gameplay telemetry (total moves, undos used, fastest solves)

This data stays on your phone and is never uploaded to remote servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The application may display optional rewarded video ads (e.g. to recharge in-game hints or undos) provided by third-party ad networks such as Google AdMob:

  • Ad providers may process anonymous, non-personal identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID, IP address, and interaction metrics) strictly for ad delivery and fraud prevention in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
  • For details on how Google processes ad data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Device Security & Integrity

Our app incorporates on-device security checks to detect rooted environments, emulators, and dynamic hooking tools (such as Frida or Xposed) to prevent cheat injection and tampering. These verifications run in memory locally on the device without transmitting diagnostic telemetry.

5. Children's Privacy

POLARITY: MAGNETIC RIFT is designed for a general audience (ages 13 and up). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete all locally stored game data at any time by navigating to Settings > Apps > POLARITY: MAGNETIC RIFT > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app from your device.
